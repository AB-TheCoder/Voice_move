The spark

Born from one frustrating analysis session.

While analysing a friendly chess game with a friend, typing every move onto a platform felt slow, irritating, and inefficient. That was enough. The old workflow had to go — replaced by a platform built for real learning, speed, and a cleaner over-the-board experience.

Chess rewards reflection. Ambitious players grow by looking back at their games. VoiceChess exists to make that reflection easy instead of tedious.

What you get

Built for the board, not the keyboard.

A prototype Android experience powered by a Python analysis backend — voice first, with score-sheet capture and engine review in the same flow.

  • Voice move recording

    Press the clock, hold, and speak your move. OpenAI Whisper turns speech into notation and writes it to the live score sheet — the feature that sells the product.

  • Built-in chess clock

    A practical timer woven into the recording flow so games stay timed without juggling extra tools.

  • Score sheet to PGN

    Capture or upload a paper score sheet. OpenCV enhances the image, OCR reads the moves, and a chess module verifies and exports clean PGN.

  • Engine analysis

    After the game, a chess engine reviews the line and surfaces output through a clear interface — so looking back becomes useful, not busywork.

How it works

Two paths into the same game file.

Voice pipeline

  1. Record the move on the clock press
  2. Transcribe with Whisper
  3. Clean notation with post-processing
  4. Validate against a live chess game loop
  5. Update the online score sheet

Image pipeline

  1. Click or upload a score-sheet photo
  2. Enhance the image before recognition
  3. Run OCR on the move columns
  4. Verify moves with chess logic
  5. Build a PGN for analysis
